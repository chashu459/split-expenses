Split It — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026

Split It is a shared expense tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What data is stored

When you use Split It, the following may be recorded: group names, member names, expense details (description, amount, who paid, how it was split, date), payment records between members, and receipt photos you choose to upload.

Where it is stored

Split It has no servers of its own and no separate database. All shared data is stored in the app owner's Google Sheet (via a Google Apps Script web app) and receipt photos in the owner's Google Drive. Your browser talks directly to those Google services — nothing passes through infrastructure we operate.

Receipt photos are shared with "anyone with the link" so that members of your group can view them.

The app also keeps one small preference in your own browser (localStorage): the "You are" identity you pick, so it remembers who you are next visit. This never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No cookies beyond what Google's own services set.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties.

Your choices

Anyone with the app link can add data to a shared group — that is the point of the app. If you want your name, expenses, or receipts removed, ask the group owner (the person hosting the sheet) to delete them.
